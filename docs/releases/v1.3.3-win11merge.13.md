# v1.3.3-win11merge.13 — Bounded texture learning across eviction

Based directly on `.11`; excludes `.12`'s experimental prepared-entry retention order. WipEout 2048 on Legion Go is the primary validation target. No new upstream/fork imports or JIT prewarming.

Automatic Texture Preparation now keeps a bounded compressed-input learning history independently of the decoded RAM cache. A texture can still be learned after its decoded copy is evicted or cleared. Valid previous profile inputs are merged before gameplay; byte-for-byte matching deduplicates inputs and updates historical cost/use information. Normal close writes the bounded merged profile atomically. No profile IO runs on demand decoding, and no decoded-pixel history is retained.

Limits: at most 64 MiB of compressed inputs plus packed-record accounting per title in the learning history, at most 16,384 records, with bounded list/index overhead. This is **extra RAM** beyond the selected decoded-cache budget. Saved profiles remain at most 64 MiB per title / 1 GiB total. Prior-profile validation still checks title/version/decoder identity, dimensions, lengths, per-input hashes and whole-file checksum before importing or decoding. Allocation pressure can drop learning observations while ordinary rendering continues; history may evict older observations at its bounds. No promise to retain every texture.

Archive selection is no longer constrained to the current decoded RAM capacity. Preparation still respects the configured decoded-cache budget and five-second deadline, skipping a record that cannot fit so smaller later records can be considered. The prepared cache uses `.11` insertion/hit promotion and ordinary demand LRU. Larger merged archives may be partially prepared; counters distinguish budget skips, merged inputs, observations, history evictions/drops and saved decoded-size estimates. No increase to the 512 MiB test cache or two-thread decode path.

Use **Settings → Performance → Automatic Texture Preparation**, play a learning session, and close normally. Later launches may reduce repeat decoding for previously observed inputs. It still prepares at game launch, not every race loading screen, and cannot prepare inputs never observed. Clearing profiles remains available when no game is running; saves/shader caches are preserved. Profiles are private game-derived data and must not be uploaded.

Validation includes eviction-before-shutdown, decoded-cache clearing, previous-profile merging, no-play sessions, title/version isolation, exact-input collision handling, disabled/read-only modes, byte/entry limits, smaller-record preparation after budget skips, concurrent observations and decoded-pixel correctness. Native self-hosted Windows suite and separate Go trial evidence must be verified before performance claims. Credits/licenses from Vita3K, Vita3K-Plus and Vita3K-nx remain preserved. Runtime and diagnostic packages share one optimized EXE; diagnostics adds its exact matching PDB and build evidence.

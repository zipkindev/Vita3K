# v1.3.3-win11merge.12 — Preserve prepared texture retention order

Texture profiles prepare their highest-ranked records first, but inserting every prepared record at the LRU front made those first records the oldest after startup. A later demand insertion could evict a high-ranked prepared texture before lower-ranked prepared textures. Append prepared records to preserve their submitted relative retention order; ordinary demand insertion and cache-hit promotion remain unchanged.

Highest-priority-first preparation, partial deadline/budget behavior, payload-byte and entry limits, decoded pixels, worker count and the UI remain unchanged. This corrects startup retention order; it does not permanently pin textures, implement cost-based demand admission, retain evicted inputs for learning, or prewarm the JIT. It may reduce avoidable re-decoding, but game-level benefit requires measurement.

Based on `.11`, retaining bounded texture reuse telemetry, automatic preparation, parallel decoding, JIT/synchronization diagnostics and the Performance settings page. The separate `.7` upstream-import candidate remains excluded. The Legion Go trial uses 512 MiB, matching optimized symbols and identical frozen learned profiles for baseline/candidate cold runs. No increase in RAM budget or worker count.

Regression coverage includes byte-cap and entry-cap priority preservation, failed partial-preload admission without eviction, telemetry classification, pixel equivalence, demand-hit promotion and ordinary LRU behavior. Dedicated Windows build/provenance and isolated Go staging are verified separately. Runtime and diagnostics packages share one optimized EXE; diagnostics adds its exact PDB and build evidence. Credits and licenses from Vita3K, Vita3K-Plus and Vita3K-nx remain preserved. No game data or learned profiles are distributed.
